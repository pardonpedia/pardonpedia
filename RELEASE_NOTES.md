# Release notes

Notable updates to Pardonpedia.

## April 6, 2026

- Improvements to the grant-date timeline chart.

## April 5, 2026

- New categories for exploring clemency by theme.
- Pardon documents open with thumbnail previews for quicker scanning.
- Clearer listing of people receiving clemency.

## April 4, 2026

- Restored Nixon-era data that was missing from the presidency chart.
- Clearer notes distinguishing pardons from commutations.
- Many grants now include warrant PDFs.

## April 1, 2026

- Replaced the judicial-district filter with **topics** for a simpler way to explore themes.
- Added new Trump-administration clemency records.
- Fixed dates and presidency coverage for older administrations, including Nixon’s first term.

## March 31, 2026

- Improved formatting for linked news stories.

## March 30, 2026

- Added related **news stories** alongside pardon entries.

## March 27, 2026

- The detail panel now reflects the person you are currently viewing.

## March 23, 2026

- Adjusted the year chart for easier reading.
- Corrected Obama-era pardon records; added links to warrant documents where available.

## March 16, 2026

- Custom domain configuration for [pardonpedia.org](https://pardonpedia.org).
- Added `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` to help search engines index the site.

## March 15, 2026

- New **About** page with project background, clemency terminology, and source links.
- Published README and a **CC-BY** data license for the dataset.
- Improved the time and presidency bar charts, including corrected term years on the president chart.
- Tidier layout and typography site-wide.
- Expanded the dataset from Justice Department clemency listings.

## March 11, 2026

- Initial public release of Pardonpedia and the starting dataset.
