# About Pardonpedia

Pardonpedia tracks presidential pardons and clemency grants issued by U.S. presidents from Nixon through the present day. It is a work in progress; we are adding new pardons as they occur and filling in details on historical ones. The primary source for this information is the Department of Justice [Office of the Pardon Attorney](https://www.justice.gov/pardon/clemency-recipients).

## Pardon Terminology

The President of the United States has the power under Article II, Section 2 of the Constitution to grant clemency — relief from a criminal conviction or sentence — in federal cases. Clemency takes several forms:

- **Pardon** — forgives a federal offense and restores civil rights
- **Commutation** — reduces a sentence without reversing the conviction
- **Reprieve** — temporarily delays the execution of a sentence
- **Remission** — reduces a fine or forfeiture
- **Amnesty** — a blanket pardon extended to a group of people

## Other Resources for Pardon Information

- **[DOJ Office of the Pardon Attorney](https://www.justice.gov/pardon)** — The official government source for clemency information, including recipient lists organized by president, aggregate statistics going back to McKinley, and the application process for those seeking clemency. The data is authoritative but bare — no context, no journalism, no easy way to search across presidencies.

- **[DOJ Clemency Statistics](https://www.justice.gov/pardon/clemency-statistics)** — A summary table of pardon and commutation counts by president, useful for historical comparison. Contains no individual-level data.

- **[Wikipedia: List of People Pardoned or Granted Clemency by the President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_people_pardoned_or_granted_clemency_by_the_president_of_the_United_States)** — A crowd-sourced, partial list of notable clemency recipients organized by president. Coverage is uneven and skews toward high-profile or controversial cases, but the entries often include useful context not found in official records.

- **[Ballotpedia: Executive Clemency and Presidential Pardons](https://ballotpedia.org/Executive_clemency_and_presidential_pardons)** — A reference overview of the clemency power, with aggregate statistics from Theodore Roosevelt forward and a running log of recent grants updated monthly.

- **[National Archives: Pardon Records](https://www.archives.gov/research/investigations/pardons)** — Holdings of historical pardon case files dating to 1853, including applications, correspondence, and recommendations from the Attorney General to the President. Essential for deep historical research, though records are incomplete for some periods.
